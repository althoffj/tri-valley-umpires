MinnDak 10U vs 12U Rule Comparison

Rule Category 10U Mid and Rec 12U Mid and Rec
Bat Rules All metal bats must have a USA Baseball stamp; wood bats allowed. Same as 10U.
Game Length 6 innings; extra innings until a winner unless ended by darkness/weather. No new inning after 1 hour 45 minutes unless both coaches agree. 6 innings; extra innings until a winner unless ended by darkness/weather. No time limit specified.
Official Game Official after 3 innings if stopped due to weather. Same as 10U.
Minimum Players At least 7 players to start. Same as 10U.
Roster and Substitutions Player on one team/roster. Can fill to 9 with lower division players (e.g., 8U), not same division/town, with opposing coach approval. Same as 10U, but lower division is 10U for 12U.
Run Limit 5-run limit per inning for first 5 innings; no limit in 6th or extra innings. Same as 10U.
Mercy Rule Game called after 4 innings (15-run lead) or 5 innings (10-run lead). Same as 10U.
Base Length 60 feet. 65 feet (or 60 feet at home team’s discretion).
Pitching Distance 46 feet 6 inches. 49 feet.
Dropped Third Strike Runners cannot advance on a dropped third strike. Runners can advance on a dropped third strike.
Stealing Bases No stealing/advancing on passed balls in May; allowed after May 31. "Home Plate Closed". Stealing/advancing on passed balls always allowed. "Home Plate Open".
Sliding Rule Runner out if does not slide or attempt to avoid fielder with ball waiting to tag. Same as 10U.
Infield Fly Not in effect. In effect.
Bunting No intentional bunting allowed. Bunting allowed.
Pitching Types No breaking pitches (called a ball if umpire catches). Breaking pitches allowed.
Switch Pitchers/Hitters Switching Arms Within Inning is allowed, switching mid at bat is forbidden. Same as 10U.
Pitching Limits 3 innings max or 75 pitches per game. 3 innings max or 85 pitches per game.
Walks No automatic intentional walks; must receive 4 pitches to walk. Same as 10U.
Batting and Fielding All players bat in order, play minimum 2 innings in field. Same as 10U.

Strike Zones

MinnDak Strike Zone – PeeWees (9-10)
Top of the shoulders to 2" below knees, 4" inside/outside of the plate.

MinnDak Strike Zone – Midgets (11-12)
Top of the shoulders to 2" below knees, 2" inside/outside of the plate.
