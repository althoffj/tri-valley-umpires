Slack Webhooks

Paste incoming webhook URLs to receive Slack notifications when games are added, updated, or cancelled, and day-of reminders at 7 AM. The Daily Game Summary channel receives a single comprehensive post at 7 AM listing all of today's games with assigned umpires and contact numbers. Leave blank to disable a channel.

Create webhooks in Slack: Apps → Incoming Webhooks → Add New Webhook to Workspace.

Manual Triggers

Send today's Slack messages immediately — useful for testing or recovering from a missed scheduled run.

Send Notification

Send a push notification to all umpires who have enabled notifications in their browser. Notifications appear even when the site is not open. If a Slack webhook is configured below, the same message will also be posted there.

Email All Umpires

Sends an email to every approved, active umpire. Use for announcements, schedule updates, or anything that needs to reach the full roster. Emails are sent individually so replies come back to you.
