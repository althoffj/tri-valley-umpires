Field Use Calendar

An iCal/ICS subscription covering field use across all facilities (games, practices, external rentals). New events are surfaced in the import tool — nothing is imported automatically. Field names are detected from the event title and matched against your configured facilities.

Send Notification

Send a push notification to all umpires who have enabled notifications in their browser. Notifications appear even when the site is not open. If a Slack webhook is configured below, the same message will also be posted there.

Sends an email to every approved, active umpire. Use for announcements, schedule updates, or anything that needs to reach the full roster. Emails are sent individually so replies come back to you.
