Send Push Notification

Send a push notification to all umpires who have enabled notifications in their browser. Notifications appear even when the site is not open. If a Slack webhook is configured, the same message will also be posted there.

Email All Umpires

Sends an email to every approved, active umpire. Use for announcements, schedule updates, or anything that needs to reach the full roster. Emails are sent individually so replies come back to you.
