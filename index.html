Getting Started

If you're new this season, follow these three steps:

  1. Review the resources — Read through the umpire expectations, core principles, rule breakdowns, and the pre-game plate meeting guide linked below.
  2. Complete the acknowledgment form — Confirm you've read and understand the expectations.
  3. Claim your games — Browse the schedule and sign up for open games.
