Tri-Valley Baseball — Season

Welcome Umpires!

Welcome to the Tri-Valley Baseball Association portal for the season. Umpires, coaches, and administrators can access schedules, field information, and league resources here.

New umpire? Complete the acknowledgment form to register. Once approved, use the ☰ menu (top right) to sign in and access the full portal.

Coach? Register as a coach to access the game schedule, request umpires, and manage your team.

Already registered? Your account requires administrator approval before you can sign in. This typically takes 1–2 days after submitting the form.

Getting Started

If you're new this season, follow these steps:

  1. Review the resources — Read through umpire expectations, core principles, rule breakdowns, and the pre-game plate meeting guide below.
  2. Claim your games — Browse the schedule and sign up for open games.

Getting Started

Welcome to the coach portal. Here's what you can do:

  • View your game schedule — See upcoming games for your division with umpire assignments.
  • Request umpires — Submit a request for additional games not already on the schedule.
  • Request practice time — Reserve a field for your team’s practices.
  • Request a call-up — Borrow a player from a younger or same-division team when yours is short-staffed. The other coach must approve.
  • File an incident report — Report ejections or conduct issues from your games.
