Tri-Valley Baseball Association
Youth Baseball Umpire Expectation Contract

Introduction

This contract outlines the expectations and responsibilities of umpires serving in the Tri-Valley Baseball Association. As an umpire, you play a critical role in ensuring fair play, safety, and a positive experience for players, coaches, and spectators. By agreeing to this contract, you commit to upholding the standards of professionalism, integrity, and respect that our association values.

Expectations and Responsibilities

1. Knowledge of Rules — https://www.minndak.org/rules/

2. Game Preparation

  • Arrive at the field at least 15 minutes before the scheduled game time.
  • Arrive with all necessary equipment gathered from the shed and return it after the game.
  • Wear age-appropriate apparel. NO shirts promoting alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or vulgar language. NO flip-flops — closed-toe shoes required.
  • Inspect the field for safety hazards and ensure equipment is properly set up.

3. Professional Conduct

  • Maintain a calm, impartial, and respectful demeanor at all times.
  • Avoid arguing with participants or engaging in confrontational behavior.
  • Use clear, concise communication when making calls.

4. Game Management and Focus

  • Enforce the rules consistently and fairly, without bias.
  • Conduct a pregame plate visit with coaches from BOTH TEAMS.
  • Manage the pace of the game to keep it on schedule.
  • Remain fully focused — avoid distractions including friends/family.

5. Safety

  • Prioritize the safety of all participants.
  • Stop play immediately if unsafe conditions arise.

6. Interaction with Participants

  • Treat players, coaches, and parents with respect.
  • Handle disputes promptly and report serious incidents immediately.

7. Cell Phone Policy

  • Prohibited during active play except for emergencies.
  • Devices must be on silent and out of sight.

8. Availability and Commitment

  • Commit to the agreed schedule and notify coordinator in advance if unavailable.
  • Find a qualified substitute if needed.

9. Compensation

  • $40 per plate game (regular season).
  • Payment at mid-season and end of season.

By umpiring for Tri-Valley Baseball, you agree to uphold these standards.
