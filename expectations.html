Tri-Valley Baseball Association Youth Baseball Umpire Expectation Contract

Introduction

This contract outlines the expectations and responsibilities of umpires serving in the Tri-Valley Baseball Association. As an umpire, you play a critical role in ensuring fair play, safety, and a positive experience for players, coaches, and spectators. By agreeing to this contract, you commit to upholding the standards of professionalism, integrity, and respect that our association values.

Expectations and Responsibilities

1. Knowledge of Rules

https://www.minndak.org/rules/

  • Umpires are expected to have a thorough understanding of the official rules of baseball as adopted by the league, including any local modifications.
  • Umpires must review the MinnDak's rulebook prior to the season and attend any provided training sessions.

2. Game Preparation

  • Arrive at the field at least 15 minutes before the scheduled game time.
  • Arrive with all necessary equipment gathered from the shed (e.g., mask, chest protector, ball/strike counter) and return at the completion of the game.
  • Wear age-appropriate apparel:
    • NO SHIRTS PROMOTING ALCOHOL, DRUGS, or TOBACCO.
    • NO SHIRTS WITH SUGGESTIVE OR VULGAR LANGUAGE.
    • NO FLIP-FLOPS! Closed-toe shoes must be worn at all bases.
    • Shorts ARE allowed.
  • Inspect the field for safety hazards and ensure game equipment (e.g., pitching mound, bases, home plate) is properly set up according to the age specifications.

3. Professional Conduct

  • Maintain a calm, impartial, and respectful demeanor at all times, regardless of the actions of players, coaches, or spectators.
  • Avoid arguing with participants or engaging in confrontational behavior.
  • Use clear, concise communication when making calls or addressing questions.

4. Game Management and Focus

  • Enforce the rules consistently and fairly, without bias toward any team.
  • Conduct a pregame plate visit with coaches from BOTH TEAMS and the base umpire.
    • Set the precedent of expected behavior.
    • Set the precedent of strike zone, as well as the third base open/closed rule.
    • Call out any field hazards or rules (e.g., Ball under backstop, Ball through the open gate in outfield).
  • Manage the pace of the game to ensure it progresses smoothly and stays on schedule.
  • Remain fully focused and attentive throughout the game:
    • Position yourself appropriately on the field.
    • Avoid distractions or lapses in concentration, including interacting with friends/family.
    • Demonstrate alertness by anticipating plays.

5. Safety

  • Prioritize the safety of all participants by monitoring weather conditions, player behavior, and equipment use.
  • Stop play immediately if unsafe conditions arise (e.g., lightning, serious injury) and follow league safety protocols.

6. Interaction with Participants

  • Treat players, coaches, and parents with respect, recognizing that this is a youth sports environment focused on development and fun.
  • Handle disputes or rule clarifications promptly and confidently, referring to association officials if necessary.
  • Responses to inappropriate behavior may include warnings, ejections, or incident reports.
  • If an individual is removed, contact the association umpire representative immediately and complete an incident report.

7. Cell Phone Policy During Games

  • Umpires are prohibited from using cell phones or other personal electronic devices for non-emergency purposes during games.
  • Cell phones may be used only during breaks for emergencies or to contact association officials.
  • Devices must be kept on silent or vibrate mode and stored out of sight while actively umpiring.

8. Availability and Commitment

  • Commit to the agreed-upon schedule and notify the coordinator at least 48 hours in advance if unable to officiate.
  • Attend any mandatory umpire meetings or training sessions.
  • Substitute Requirement: You are responsible for finding a qualified substitute and notifying the coordinator at least 24 hours prior.

9. Compensation

  • Regular season: $40 per plate game (single plate umpire only; base umpires are parent volunteers and unpaid).
  • Payment by check at mid-season and end of season, contingent on fulfilling responsibilities.
  • Tournament pay determined by tournament officials.

By umpiring for Tri-Valley Baseball, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to all the above expectations.
