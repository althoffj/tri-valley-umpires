Request an Umpire for Your Game

Use this form to request umpire coverage for a non-league or tournament game. An administrator will review your request and reach out to confirm availability.

Note: For non-league games, coaches are responsible for payment directly to umpires. Standard pay rates are Plate: $40 / Field: $30.
