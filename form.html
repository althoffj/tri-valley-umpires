Umpire Rules & Expectations Acknowledgment

Tri-Valley Baseball Association Youth Baseball Umpire Expectation Contract

Introduction
This contract outlines the expectations and responsibilities of umpires serving in the Tri-Valley Baseball Association. As an umpire, you play a critical role in ensuring fair play, safety, and a positive experience for players, coaches, and spectators. By agreeing to this contract, you commit to upholding the standards of professionalism, integrity, and respect that our association values.

Expectations and Responsibilities

1. Knowledge of Rules — https://www.minndak.org/rules/

2. Game Preparation
• Arrive at the field at least 15 minutes before scheduled start.
• Bring and return all equipment properly.
• Wear appropriate apparel (no alcohol, drugs, tobacco, or vulgar language). Closed-toe shoes required.
• Inspect field for safety.

3. Professional Conduct
• Maintain calm, impartial, respectful demeanor.
• Avoid arguing or confrontational behavior.

4. Game Management and Focus
• Enforce rules fairly.
• Conduct pregame meeting with both teams.
• Keep game on schedule and stay fully focused.

5. Safety
• Prioritize participant safety and stop play if needed.

6. Interaction with Participants
• Treat everyone with respect.
• Report incidents promptly.

7. Cell Phone Policy
• No non-emergency use during games.

8. Availability & Commitment
• Honor schedule and find substitutes if necessary.

9. Compensation
• $40 per plate game (regular season).

By submitting this form you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to all the above expectations.
