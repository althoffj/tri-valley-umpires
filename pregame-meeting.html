Conducting the Pre-Game Plate Meeting

Start the meeting 5 minutes before game time. Ensure all coaches attend — this meeting is non-optional.

Suggested Script

“Good afternoon, I’m your umpire today. Please address me as Blue, Sir, or Umpire. I am human, and will do the best that I can to make the calls as I see them. Let’s go over the ground rules.”

Player & Team Conduct

  • Ensure players’ jerseys are tucked, and they demonstrate good sportsmanship.
  • Remind your team to hustle on and off the field in order to keep the game moving.
  • Pitchers get 5 warm-up pitches between innings, 8 for fresh pitchers.

Code of Conduct & Discipline

  • Swearing or throwing equipment results in an ejection.
  • Arguing judgment calls or balls/strikes is not allowed.
  • For rule clarifications, bring the rulebook between innings for a calm discussion.
  • Ranting coaches will be returned to the dugout; continued disruption leads to ejection, and play will stop until the offending coach has left the field.
  • Coaches, handle disruptive spectators. Persistent issues pause the game until resolved.

Strike Zone

Division Vertical Horizontal
10U 2 inches below knees to top of shoulders 4 inches either side of the plate
12U 2 inches below knees to top of shoulders 2 inches either side of the plate

Field & Ground Rules

Review with both coaches before the first pitch:

  • Field boundaries — where is in play vs. out of play?
  • Dead ball areas — fences, dugouts, parked cars, etc.
  • Any local ground rules specific to this field.

Closing Statement

“Let’s have a safe, fun game! Good luck to both teams.”
