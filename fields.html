General Notes

  • Arrive 20–30 minutes early to set up bases and check field conditions before the coaches' plate meeting.
  • Restrooms are available at both complexes near the concession stands.
  • Parking — use the main lot at each complex. Do not park on the grass near the outfield fences.
  • If a field is unplayable (standing water, poor conditions), contact Jeff Althoff at 605-380-0229 before the game time.
  • Equipment bags (bases, lineup cards) are stored in the concession stand / scorer's booth at each complex.
